Lagos Craftsmanship, Refined

At Atelier Haute, we reject the frantic pace of fast fashion. Our approach is unhurried, rooted in the deep-seated traditions of Nigerian tailoring while embracing a global contemporary silhouette.

Every garment tells a story of heritage. From the hand-loomed fibers of our Aso Oke to the Italian linens we source, each material is selected for its integrity and tactile quality. We don't just make clothes; we craft legacies.

The Journey to
Precision

Our process is a dialogue between tailor and client, spanning approximately six to eight weeks of careful construction.

01. Consultation

A private session to discuss lifestyle, preferences, and fabric selection in our Ikoyi atelier.

02. Measurement

Over 30 distinct measurements are taken to create a unique paper pattern exclusive to your frame.

03. The First Fitting

The 'baste fitting' where the garment is temporary stitched to refine the balance and drape on your body.

04. Completion

Final hand-finishing, buttonhole stitching, and the final reveal of your bespoke commission.
