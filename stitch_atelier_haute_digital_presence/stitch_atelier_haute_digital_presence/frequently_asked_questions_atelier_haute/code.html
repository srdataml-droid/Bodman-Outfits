Ordering & Process

Our signature bespoke process typically spans 6 to 10 weeks. This timeframe accounts for the selection of heritage fabrics, initial cutting, and at least two distinct fitting sessions to ensure absolute precision. For hand-woven Agbada or intricate lace garments, we recommend allowing 12 weeks.
While we take pride in our curated selection of heritage silks, wools, and hand-loomed textiles, we do occasionally work with client-provided fabric for sentimental pieces. A consultation is required to assess the fabric's suitability for the desired design.

Pricing & Payment

Yes. For bespoke orders exceeding a certain threshold, we offer a structured payment plan consisting of a 50% initial deposit to begin the pattern-making process, and the remaining 50% due upon the final fitting before garment release.
Your consultation fee is a commitment to the design journey. Should you choose to proceed with a bespoke order, the full consultation fee is credited toward the final cost of your garment.

Measurements & Fitting

While we prefer an in-person measurement session at our Lagos or London studios, we offer a "Virtual Tailor" service. This involves a guided video call with our lead fitter to ensure measurements are taken accurately.

Delivery & Timelines

Indeed. Atelier Haute serves a global clientele. We use premium couriers (DHL/FedEx) to ensure your garment arrives in pristine condition, housed in our bespoke garment bags and flight-ready packaging.

Alterations & Care

Hand-woven textiles like Aso Oke should never be hung for extended periods as the weight can distort the weave. We recommend folding these garments with acid-free tissue paper and storing them in the Atelier Haute cedar trunk provided at purchase.
